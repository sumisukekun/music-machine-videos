Ball Music Studio

Ball Music Studio is a small personal tool by BusinessMaker. It renders short music videos with Blender — balls fired by machines hit instruments in time with public-domain music — and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok account (@ball.music855) through the TikTok Content Posting API.

Ball Music Studio は BusinessMaker の個人用ツールです。パブリックドメインの名曲に合わせて、機械が打ち出した玉が楽器を鳴らすショート動画を Blender で作り、作成者自身の TikTok アカウント（@ball.music855）へ TikTok Content Posting API で投稿します。

How it works / 仕組み

  • The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the video.publish scope for their own account only.
  • Videos are rendered automatically and posted on a schedule with the privacy setting the creator chose. No other users' data is collected.
  • The creator can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions).
